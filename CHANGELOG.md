# Meio Shards

## 1.3.0

- Clicking the counter or pressing the key in combat or a boss fight no longer causes an
  error: it says "not in combat", and you click again after the fight
- `/shards trim` is quiet, so it fits in your Drain Soul macro
- Shows on Titan Panel, ChocolateBar and other data broker bars: count, tooltip, click to
  delete extras, right click for settings
- `/shards help` is shorter and says where to set the delete key
- New, off by default: `/shards auto on` deletes extras by themselves on your next key
  press or click, never in combat or the shards you brought. Not tested in a live game yet,
  so it starts off; tell us how it goes

## 1.2.1

- First release: a Soul Shard counter with a cap, a delete key for the extras and a warning
  when you run low
